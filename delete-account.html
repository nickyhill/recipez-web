Delete your RecipEZ account

For the RecipEZ app on Android and iOS

You can ask us to permanently delete your RecipEZ account and all the data linked to it. You do not need the app installed to make a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the address you used to sign up for RecipEZ to YOUR-EMAIL@example.com with the subject "Delete my RecipEZ account". Include your username if you remember it. You can use the form below to write the email for you.
  2. If the request comes from a different address than the one on the account, we will reply to the account's email address to confirm before deleting anything.
  3. We delete the account within 30 days and email you when it is done.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Email address, username and passwordDeleted within 30 days
Recipes, including notes, nutrition details, categories, bookmarks and linksDeleted within 30 days
Menus, cookbooks and foldersDeleted within 30 days
Copies in Supabase backupsExpire within an additional 30 days
Data saved on your own deviceRemoved when you sign out or uninstall the app

We do not keep any of your data after deletion, except where the law requires us to.

Delete recipes without deleting your account

To remove your recipes, menus or folders but keep your account, open the RecipEZ app and go to Settings. Deleting them there removes them from your device and from our servers right away.
