RecipEZ Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Introduction

RecipEZ collects only what it needs to give you an account and save your recipes. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

This policy explains what the RecipEZ mobile app ("RecipEZ," "we," "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We collect two kinds of information, both of which you give us directly.

  • Account information. Your email address, the username you choose and a password when you sign up. Your username is shown in the app's Settings. Your password is stored only in hashed form; we never see or store it in plain text.
  • Your recipe collection. The recipes you add, import or edit, including titles, descriptions, ingredients, instructions, cook times, servings, notes, nutrition details, categories, the recipe's source link and image link, and which recipes you bookmark or last opened.
  • How you organize recipes. The menus, cookbooks and folders you create, and which recipes are in each.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and we do not use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools. If you import a recipe file, such as a Paprika export, the file is read on your device and only the recipes in it are saved.

How we use your information

We use your information only to run the app:

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep it secure
  • To save your recipes and sync them across the devices where you sign in
  • To send you account emails you ask for, such as email confirmation or a password reset
  • To respond when you contact us for help

We do not send marketing emails, and we do not use your recipes to train AI models or build profiles about you.

Where your data is stored

Your account and recipes are stored with Supabase, which provides our database and sign-in service. Supabase processes this data on our behalf and only to provide that service.

Supabase's servers may be located outside your country. When data is transferred, it is protected by Supabase's security measures and contractual safeguards.

A copy of your recipes, menus, cookbooks and folders is also kept on your device so the app works offline, and your sign-in session is saved on your device so you stay logged in. Signing out ends the session.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. Your recipes are private to your account. We share data only with Supabase to run the app, or when the law requires it, such as a valid court order.

Recipe websites. When you import a recipe from a web link, your device loads that page directly from the website, and recipe images load from the sites that host them. Recipes without a photo show a stand-in image loaded from the placeholder service via.placeholder.com. Those websites can see your device's IP address, as with any web visit, and their own privacy policies apply. We don't send them your email or account details.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and recipes for as long as your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your email, password and recipes from our database within 30 days. Backups held by Supabase may take up to an additional 30 days to expire.

Deleting your account

You can delete all your recipes, menus or folders at any time in the app's Settings. This removes them from your device and from our servers. To delete your account entirely, including your email address, submit a deletion request or email us at the address below and we will do it within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent between the app and Supabase over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to each user's data is restricted to that user's account. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your information by contacting us. Depending on where you live, such as the EU, UK or California, you may have additional rights under local law, and you can raise a concern with your local data protection authority.

Children

RecipEZ is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app or by email.
